From download to dictation

Three steps. Then it stays out of the way.

  1. 01

    Install

    Open the DMG and drag whisper_hotkey into Applications. The app, local recognition engine, and Base English model arrive together.

  2. 02

    Allow

    Open the app once and follow its setup window for Microphone, Accessibility, and Input Monitoring permissions.

  3. 03

    Speak

    Focus any text field, hold Right Command, speak, and release. The final text is inserted at the field you chose.

A deliberately short data path

Nothing to sign into.
Nothing to send away.

whisper_hotkey records only while you dictate. Recognition runs locally with whisper.cpp, the temporary recording is deleted, and the transcript goes directly to the focused app.
